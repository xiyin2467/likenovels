The vow beneath the moon

Mara should have turned back when the corridor lamps dimmed, but the scent of cedar smoke and winter rain had already found her.

Behind the carved black door, the Alpha's study waited in silence. Every wolf in the northern pack knew that room was forbidden.

Kael stood in the amber spill of the firelight, coat unbuttoned, silver eyes fixed on her mouth as if he had been listening to her breathe.

"You should not be here," he said. His voice was low enough to feel like a warning and close enough to feel like a promise.
